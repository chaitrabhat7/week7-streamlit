Archimedes — AI Maths Tutor for CBSE Students

The Problem

A maths tutor with a class of 7 students cannot be available for every doubt that arises during homework. When stuck, students typically turn to generic internet searches or general-purpose AI tools that either hand over answers directly or use teaching styles misaligned with their tutor's approach. This undermines learning and creates dependency on shortcuts rather than understanding.

The Solution

Archimedes is a custom AI maths tutor built specifically for CBSE Class 8–10 students. It replicates the tutor's exact teaching style — using a combination of direct explanation for new concepts and the Socratic method for familiar ones, with real-world relatable examples. It never hands over answers directly; it guides students to arrive at solutions themselves.

How It Works

Built with Python, Streamlit, and the Claude API. The tutor uploads a chapter PDF from the sidebar — Archimedes then answers strictly from that content, ensuring focused, syllabus-aligned practice. Conversation memory means the bot remembers context across the session. The subject scope is locked to CBSE Maths, so students cannot drift into unrelated topics — making it safe for unsupervised use.

The Result

Students can now practice independently at home, get their doubts cleared instantly, and request additional practice questions — all within the boundaries their tutor has set. Parents can hand over access without concern. The tutor's teaching style and pedagogy are preserved even when she isn't in the room.
